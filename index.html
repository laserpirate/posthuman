    Contrast
    
        
    Applied on the output after the scene or sketch, on the controller and in the output window. Hydras and Kodamas each keep their own. P cycles styles. A playlist row's Pal button gives that item its own palette; while it is on screen, these controls edit it.

    Scene controls
    
          
    
          
    
          
    Speed
    
          
    Turntable
    
          
    View
    
          
    
            
    
          
    Speed scales the whole scene clock (0.1× to 3×). Turntable 0 stops the orbit. Orbit, pan and zoom: drag on the stage or on the pad (shift-drag or right-drag pans, wheel zooms), here or in the output window; the turntable rests while you steer and resumes 2 s later. A playlist row's View button saves the current view for that item.
